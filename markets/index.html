Primary Markets

Built for the platforms where reliability is non-negotiable.

Brochure market definitions — aerospace, defence and naval & marine as the three primary markets.

Aerospace

Airliners, business jets, and military fixed-wing and rotary aircraft. Airframe, engine, nacelle, wing, thrust-reverser and refuelling electrics, flight-test instrumentation and IFE harnesses.

Defence

Military platforms, defence vehicles and communications. Rugged harnesses, RF assemblies and box builds. JOSCAR registered for defence supply chains.

Naval & Marine

Cable assemblies, sub-assemblies and box build. Quality systems in accordance with DEF STAN 05-97; data managed in line with DEFCON 659.
