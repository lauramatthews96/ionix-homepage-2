Harnesses, engineered to the platform

Flying today

On leading long-haul commercial aircraft and business jets.

Every harness is engineered to your cost targets, environment and installation constraints. It's designed to save weight, simplify installation and reduce long-term maintenance. In-house overbraiding adds protection where heat and wear demand it. Our power cable assemblies are built in thousands every week and go into aerospace, electric vehicles, generator sets, wind-turbine converters and rail.

Electrical Test

Electrically tested. Built for reliability.

MK Test automatic electrical test systems check completed wiring harnesses and electrical assemblies for faults. They do not manufacture the cables — they verify continuity, resistance, short circuits and insulation resistance before shipment.

Every harness and cordset is functionally tested on the floor, with the result accountable on the station monitor.
