Hand and automated cut, strip, terminate and seal work feeds every harness build. Crimping stations keep gauge, die and force under control so terminations meet the drawing before the assembly moves to test.

Component Storage

Kardex vertical lift — organised parts, not cable manufacture.

A Kardex Shuttle is an automated storage and retrieval system. It does not manufacture cables. It stores and presents the components production needs — connectors, terminals, wire, cable accessories and other small parts — so kits and builds start with the right item, at the right time.

For Ionix, that keeps inventory dense, traceable and ready for harness, RF and fibre work without cluttering the floor with open shelving.

Electrical Verification

MK Test — faults found before shipment, not after fit.

MK Test automatic electrical test systems check completed wiring harnesses and assemblies for continuity, resistance, shorts and insulation faults. They verify the build; they do not manufacture the cable.
