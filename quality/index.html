MK Test Systems

Innovative automatic electrical test systems

MK Test equipment checks completed wiring harnesses and electrical assemblies for faults. It does not manufacture the cables — it verifies continuity, resistance, short circuits and insulation resistance, depending on the model.

Ionix runs a bank of MK testers on the floor alongside Adaptronic, Cirris and Ditmco stations, so every build can be electrically proven before it ships.
