Who we are · IX·02

Interconnect you can stake a programme on.

High-reliability wiring harnesses and interconnect assemblies for aerospace, defence and other demanding sectors.

Discuss Your Programme Our Story

We design and manufacture high-reliability wiring harnesses and interconnect assemblies for aerospace, defence and other demanding sectors. Ionix Systems is a specialist manufacturer of advanced interconnect: wiring harnesses, power, fibre optic and RF assemblies, and box builds. We support customers from prototype through to production and test. We engineer and industrialise in the UK and manufacture at volume in Estonia and India. As part of Amphenol, one of the world's largest interconnect manufacturers, we pair specialist expertise with global strength.

Rolls-Royce Supplier of the Year 2021 — Founder member, Rolls-Royce High Performing Supplier Group · 2026 member

Customers include Rolls-Royce, BAE Systems and Rheinmetall.
